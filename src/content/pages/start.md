Everything on the rest of this site is aimed at leaders and organizations. This section is aimed at you: the person actually living through the workload, the reorganizations, the new tools, and the quiet, nagging question of what it all means for you. So let's start honestly.

Two out of three employees reported experiencing some form of burnout last year. Four in ten now worry AI could make their role obsolete — nearly double the share who felt that way just two years ago. If you recognize either of those feelings, you are describing a widely shared, well-documented experience, not a personal shortfall. Researchers even have a name for the specific exhaustion of living through wave after wave of workplace change: **change fatigue** — and it happens to your nervous system, not because you're insufficiently resilient.

### What you'll find here

- **[Your Coping Toolkit](/toolkit/)** — practical, research-backed ways to manage everyday burnout and stress, built around understanding what's really driving your particular version of it.
- **[Navigating AI Anxiety, Personally](/aicope/)** — a more specific guide if what you're carrying is less "generally overwhelmed" and more "uneasy about what AI means for my job and my skills."
